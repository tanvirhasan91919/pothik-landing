Questions?

Still stuck? Message us on WhatsApp. A real person in Dhaka replies, day or night.

Can I get a refund if plans change?

Cancel 7 or more days before departure and you get everything back, except the ৳1,000 reservation, which becomes trip credit for 12 months. Cancel 3 to 6 days before and you get 50% back. Under 72 hours we can't refund, but you can hand your seat to a friend for free. If we cancel because of weather, permits or a hartal, you get 100% back to your bKash within 3 working days.

How does paying in parts with bKash work?

Reserve your seat with ৳1,000. Pay the rest in up to 3 parts on bKash, Nagad, Rocket or card, with the last part due 72 hours before departure. We send a WhatsApp reminder before each one. No interest and no extra charge.

What's included in the price?

Return transport from Dhaka, every night's stay, the meals listed on the trip, entry fees and permits, a Pothik trip leader and local guides. Not included: your own snacks, optional activities like paragliding in Pokhara, and tips. Every trip lists both, line by line.

Is it safe? Who are the guides?

Every group has a trip leader trained in first aid and a licensed local guide. Mixed groups always have a female co-leader, and rooms are split by gender unless you book as a couple or a group of friends. Our 24/7 WhatsApp line reaches a real person in Dhaka, in Bangla or English.

Do groups get a discount?

Book 10 seats and the 10th is free; book 20 and two are free. The saving is split across the group so everyone pays less. Groups of 5 to 9 get ৳300 off per person. Ask for a quote and we reply on WhatsApp within 2 hours, 9am to 11pm.
